## Actual implementation
This is a Blender (Cycles) **source-scene model**, not an AI concept board and not deformation of raster pixels. It was generated by `experiments/orbis-blender-sculpt-20261127/scene.py`. The approved original Orbis image was used **only for visual registration of curve coordinates**, not as texture/UV displace input.

## Visual gates
| Gate | Result | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Real editable 3D geometry | PASS | Source `.blend` contains 9 separate mesh layers + glass-shell sphere |
| Source raster used as surface animation | PASS (not used) | No texture/warping of concept image |
| Main boundaries tracked from approved image | PARTIAL | Better relationship of broad sweeps than freeform v06, but the result is still mostly 4 large projected leaf-like shapes |
| Sculptural interleaving and true folded relief | **FAIL** | Many layers overlap without creating the target's sophisticated spatial folding, rhythmic volume and clearly perceived depth |
| Ivory optical membrane character | **FAIL** | Excessive glossy/specular response causes patchy shiny highlights and harsh contrast, rather than broad delicate pearlescence |
| Burgundy precise inner current | **FAIL** | Large magenta area remains visually dominant in geometry; the red signature line alone is not integrated into a rich interior |
| Outer glass shell / optics | **FAIL** | Edge is present, but complete optical depth and layered refraction are not credible or close enough to approved CGI |
| Light theme | **FAIL** | Pale washed-out components and weak interior separation |
| Smooth animation | Not attempted | Static gate failed. Rigging, motion sequence and FPS must not be used to hide these failures |

## Root cause and decision
Simply moving from 4 to 9 parametrically authored patches, raising Cycles samples or adding layered shaders **does not restore the missing human-directed topology**. The approved image shows deeply nested, carefully folded volumetric sheets whose hidden surfaces and illumination cannot be uniquely recovered from a single view. The shape itself is the priority blocker, followed by optical art direction.

**Stop all additional generic spline + shader variants.** Treat this scene only as a boundary-registration and source-building tool, not as the production Orbis. Meaningful next work requires careful DCC authoring of the actual 3D mesh, with direct per-face/per-surface visual review, or specialist sculpting input. If that source is obtained and its still frame is approved in light and dark, then integrate controlled geometry-rig animation and evaluate a Cycles-rendered clip vs live GPU.

The concurrent Ribbon experiment was neither changed nor reused. KONSTANTIN's app, existing Render previews, and previously rejected experiments were left intact.
